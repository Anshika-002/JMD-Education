## 🎓 About JMD Education Worldwide

</div>

**JMD Education Worldwide** is an education consultancy platform focused
on helping students explore opportunities to pursue **MBBS abroad**.

The platform provides information and guidance for students interested
in studying medicine in countries including:

- 🇷🇺 **Russia**
- 🇬🇪 **Georgia**
- 🇺🇿 **Uzbekistan**
- 🇰🇿 **Kazakhstan**

Our goal is to make the journey from researching universities to
understanding the admission process easier for students.
